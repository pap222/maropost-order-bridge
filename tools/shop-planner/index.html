In the shop

Move a table: click it, then drag it across the floor. It snaps to a 25 cm grid. Press R to turn it 15°, Shift+R for 90°.

Move produce: drag a bed of produce onto another bed to swap the two. Or drag a produce card from the list straight onto a bed.

Prices: click a bed and edit the price card on the right. Cards render in the 3D view.

Camera: drag empty floor to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan. Top-down gives a plan view.

Save: type a name and Save. Saved layouts are shared with everyone who opens this page. Your working copy also autosaves in this browser.

Room is 26 × 16 m and fixtures are placed from the photos, so treat sizes as approximate until you've measured and adjusted them in the inspector.

Click a table or bed to select it. Drag tables to move, drag beds to swap produce. R rotates.
