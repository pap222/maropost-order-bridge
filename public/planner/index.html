In the shop

Move a table: click it, then drag it across the floor. It snaps to a 25 cm grid. Press R to turn it 15°, Shift+R for 90°.

Move produce: drag a bed of produce onto another bed to swap the two. Or drag a produce card from the list straight onto a bed.

Prices: click a bed and edit the price card on the right. Cards render in the 3D view.

Camera: drag empty floor to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan. The round buttons at the bottom right turn and zoom the view. Top-down gives a plan view.

On a phone: tap a table, then use the 15° and 90° buttons that appear to turn it, and Details to change what's on it.

Save: type a name and Save. Saved layouts are shared with everyone who opens this page. Your working copy also autosaves in this browser.

Plan underlay: the Plan button lays the architect's drawing on the floor. Top-down view plus Plan is the quickest way to check a table sits where it should.

Walls, lounges, shelves and gondolas are placed at their drawn dimensions. Two changes from the drawing as built: the entry lounges are split in two, and the grocery gondolas are linked into a snake. Move anything that differs on the day.

Click a table or bed to select it. Drag tables to move, drag beds to swap produce. R rotates. Zoom in to see price cards.
